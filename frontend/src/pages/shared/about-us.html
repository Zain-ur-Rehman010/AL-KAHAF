FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about Al-Kahaf Online Academy and our Islamic learning programs.

What courses does Al-Kahaf Online Academy offer?

We offer a comprehensive range of Islamic courses including Noorani Qaida, Quran Recitation with Tajweed, Hifz-ul-Quran (Quran Memorization), Islamic Studies, Arabic Language, and more. All courses are taught by certified Islamic scholars.

Are your instructors qualified Islamic scholars?

Yes. All our instructors hold authentic ijazah in their respective subjects and have graduated from recognized Islamic institutions. We thoroughly verify credentials before onboarding any instructor.

Can children join the courses?

Absolutely. We have courses designed for children from age 5 and above. A parent or guardian will need to create the account. Our instructors are specifically trained to teach young learners in a comfortable and engaging way.

What are the course fees and payment options?

Course fees vary by program and are priced in PKR. We offer flexible monthly and semester-based plans. Payments can be made via JazzCash or card. We also have a Welfare Program for students who cannot afford fees — contact us to apply.

Will I receive a certificate after completing a course?

Yes. Upon successful completion, you will receive a verified digital certificate from Al-Kahaf Online Academy, available for download directly from your student dashboard.
