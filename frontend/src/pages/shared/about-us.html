Our Story

A Legacy of Authentic Islamic Education

Al-Kahaf Online Academy was founded in 2019 by Pir Zulfiqar Ahmed Naqshbandi (رحمة الله علیه) with the vision of modernizing Islamic learning through technology. Today, the academy operates under the supervision of Hafiz Ibrahim Naqshbandi, continuing the legacy of providing structured and high-quality Islamic education.

Based in Lahore, Pakistan, we offer a complete range of courses — from Noorani Qaida to advanced Dars-e-Nizami, Arabic language, Hifz programs, and specialized short courses for children and adults.

Since its inception, Al-Kahaf Online Academy has helped thousands of students strengthen their Quranic knowledge, memorize the Quran, and connect deeply with Islamic teachings — all from the comfort of their homes.

Mission & Vision

What Drives Us Forward

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide high-quality Islamic education online, making it accessible to students of all ages worldwide. We aim to nurture strong Quranic knowledge, correct Tajweed, Hifz, and Arabic skills, while promoting Islamic values and character development in a safe and supportive environment.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a globally recognized online Islamic academy that empowers students to excel in religious studies, promote Islamic knowledge worldwide, and create a generation of confident, knowledgeable, and ethical individuals rooted in Islamic teachings.

Your Questions Answered

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about learning at Al-Kahaf Online Academy.

Do you offer Arabic language courses?

Yes, we provide Arabic courses that teach speaking, reading, and writing from basic to advanced levels, suitable for all students.

How long does it take to complete the Dars-e-Nizami course?

The Dars-e-Nizami program is designed to be completed over approximately eight years.

Can students join from outside the country?

Yes, our online system supports students worldwide.

Where is your academy located?

Our academy is based in Lahore, Pakistan, and it offers complete online education accessible from anywhere.

Is your academy only for male students?

Our academy primarily serves male students. However, young girls aged 10–12 can participate in some courses.
