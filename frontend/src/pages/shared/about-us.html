Your Questions Answered

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about learning at Al-Kahaf Online Academy.

How do I book a free trial class?

Simply visit our Free Trial page, fill in your name, contact details, and preferred time slot. Our team will reach out within 24 hours to confirm your session with a certified instructor.

What is the class schedule and how flexible is it?

Classes are fully flexible and scheduled around your availability. You can choose morning, afternoon, or evening slots, 7 days a week. Once you select a time, it becomes your recurring weekly slot with the same instructor.

Do I need prior knowledge to start learning Quran?

No prior knowledge is required. We welcome complete beginners, including young children. During the free trial, your instructor will assess your level and place you in the right course — whether that's Noorani Qaida or an advanced Tajweed program.

Are classes one-on-one or in a group?

We offer both one-on-one and small group sessions. One-on-one classes are recommended for faster progress and personalized feedback, while group classes are a more affordable option for families or friends learning together.

What happens if I miss a class?

We understand life can be unpredictable. If you need to miss a class, inform your instructor at least 2 hours in advance and we will reschedule it at a convenient time. Missed classes are not deducted from your enrolled sessions.

Do you offer a Welfare Program for students in need?

Yes. Al-Kahaf Online Academy has a dedicated Welfare Program for students who cannot afford course fees. We believe Islamic education should be accessible to everyone. You can apply through our Projects page or contact us directly to discuss your situation confidentially.
