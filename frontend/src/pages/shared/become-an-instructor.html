The Process

How to Get Started

From application to your first student — here's what the journey looks like.

1
Submit Your Application

Fill out the form below with your background, specialization, and why you want to teach. Takes less than 5 minutes.

2
Application Review

Our team reviews your application within 2–3 business days and contacts you if we need more information.

3
Account Activation

Upon approval, you'll receive login credentials for your instructor dashboard. No extra setup needed.

4
Start Teaching & Earning

Create your first course, publish it with admin approval, and start earning as students enroll.

What Happens Next?
  • We review your application within 2–3 business days.
  • You'll receive an email with the decision.
  • On approval, your instructor login is created automatically.
  • Our team will schedule a brief onboarding call.
Who Should Apply?
  • Subject matter experts in any field
  • Quran / Islamic Studies teachers
  • IT, programming, design professionals
  • Language teachers (Urdu, Arabic, English)
  • Business, finance & entrepreneurship mentors
CV Tips
  • Keep it to 1–2 pages — concise wins.
  • Highlight your teaching or training experience first.
  • List relevant certifications and qualifications.
  • Upload as PDF for best compatibility.
  • Make sure your contact email matches the form.
Already Registered?

If you already have a student account, we'll upgrade it to instructor on approval — no new account needed.
